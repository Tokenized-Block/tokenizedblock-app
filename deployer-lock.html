Deploy the TBLOCK lock (Base)

One contract, one signature. TBlockLock: a holder locks their OWN TBLOCK for at least 7 days; to leave, they request the withdrawal (the perks stop at once) and take it all back 7 days later — nobody else can. No owner, no fee, no pause, no rescue: whoever deploys gets no right over it. The app's holder features read what is locked (activeLockedOf), never a balance read at one instant.
