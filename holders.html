What this means

TBLOCK has no burn function and its supply never shrinks. What sits at 0x…dEaD, an address nobody holds the key of, is out of circulation for good. The pool's hook sends a cut of each trade there, every heart burns its price there, and each contest vote sends its fee there.

What the proof shows, and what it does not
  • It proves the wallet held enough TBLOCK when you asked, and that you control it (ordinary and smart wallets).
  • The invite works once and expires after 15 minutes; one invite per wallet per day. It does not check that you keep holding after joining.
  • TBLOCK can lose value. Holding it is not an investment we promise anything on.
